    What about the Eiffel Tower?

    AI Summary

    The Eiffel Tower is an iconic landmark located in Paris, France. It was completed in 1889 and stands approximately 330 meters tall, making it one of the tallest structures in the city. Here are some key details about the Eiffel Tower:
    Construction and Design
    • Designed by engineer Gustave Eiffel, the tower was initially criticized but has become a symbol of French culture.
    • It was constructed for the 1889 Exposition Universelle (World's Fair) to celebrate the 100th anniversary of the French Revolution.
    Visitor Experience
    • The tower attracts millions of visitors each year, offering stunning views of Paris from its observation decks.
    • Visitors can access three levels, with restaurants and shops available on the first two levels.
    Cultural Significance
    • The Eiffel Tower has appeared in numerous films, artworks, and literature, solidifying its status as a global icon.
    • It is often illuminated at night, creating a beautiful spectacle for both tourists and locals.
