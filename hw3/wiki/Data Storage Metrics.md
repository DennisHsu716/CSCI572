# Data Storage Metrics

> [!abstract] Summary
> Standardized units of digital data capacity used to quantify the exponential growth and storage requirements of the internet.

## Unit Hierarchies
To manage the vast data footprint of the modern web, the industry utilizes a base-1024 metric progression. Understanding these scales is crucial for planning infrastructure:
* **Petabyte (PB):** $1,024 	imes$ Terabytes.
* **Exabyte (EB):** $1,024 	imes$ Petabytes.
* **Zettabyte (ZB):** $1,024 	imes$ Exabytes.

## Practical Application
These units are not merely theoretical; they are daily operational requirements for high-scale environments. For example, Google processes daily data volumes measured in petabytes. Similarly, storing the historical snapshots of the web requires immense capacity—an archive of one trillion pages at an average size of 100KB per page translates to roughly 100 Petabytes of storage. As the costs for storage have dropped significantly (with some projections placing 1 Petabyte under $1,000 at the time of reporting), the ability to store vast swathes of human knowledge has become increasingly feasible.
