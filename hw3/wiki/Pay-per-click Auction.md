# Pay-per-click Auction

> [!abstract] Summary
> A mechanism introduced by Goto.com that allows advertisers to bid for ad positions, paying only when a user interacts with the link (p. 2).

## Mechanism and Efficiency
The pay-per-click (PPC) auction revolutionized the search industry by replacing flat-fee arrangements with dynamic bidding. Introduced by Goto.com, this model allowed advertisers to bid for specific ad positions. The key innovation was the performance-based nature of the payment; advertisers pay only when a user actually clicks on their link (p. 2).

This shift was significantly more efficient than the older [[Paid Inclusion Model]], as it aligned the cost of advertising directly with actual user engagement (p. 12). Because the market determines the price for keywords, the model naturally optimizes for high-performing ads, making it the industry standard for search engine revenue generation (p. 30).
