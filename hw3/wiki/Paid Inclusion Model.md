# Paid Inclusion Model

> [!abstract] Summary
> A search engine monetization strategy where website owners pay a fee to guarantee that their site appears in search results for specific terms (p. 12).

## Business Dynamics
In the earlier stages of search engine development, finding sustainable revenue was a significant challenge. The paid inclusion model allowed companies like Inktomi to generate income by charging site owners for guaranteed placement within search indices (p. 12). While this model provided immediate revenue, it eventually faced criticism regarding the ethical implications of allowing payments to influence search rankings, potentially biasing the quality of results (p. 12, p. 31).

## Historical Comparisons
Before the dominance of more efficient models, engines experimented with various financial tactics, including basic banner advertisements (p. 30). Eventually, the paid inclusion model became less competitive compared to the newer, more automated auction systems that better aligned advertiser value with user demand.
